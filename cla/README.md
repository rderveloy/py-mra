# Contributor License Agreements

py-mra is published under [AGPL-3.0-or-later](../LICENSE) and is also
available under a separate commercial license. For both tracks to work,
the project maintainer needs the legal right to license contributions
under either license, including for commercial use.

This directory holds the agreements contributors are asked to accept.

## Which one applies to you

- If you are contributing on your own behalf — i.e. the code is yours,
  not your employer's — sign the [Individual CLA](./individual.md).
- If you are contributing on behalf of an employer (the code was written
  in the course of your employment, your employer has rights in the
  work, or any similar arrangement applies), your employer signs the
  [Corporate CLA](./corporate.md) and lists you as an authorized
  contributor.

If both could apply, you almost certainly need the Corporate version.
When in doubt, ask your employer or err on the side of the Corporate CLA.

## How to sign

Until the CLA-tracking automation is fully wired up, please include a
line in the description of your first pull request:

> I have read [`cla/individual.md`](./individual.md) (or
> [`cla/corporate.md`](./corporate.md)) and agree to its terms.

Once the CLA bot is installed on the repository, it will prompt new
contributors to confirm agreement on their first PR and block the merge
until they have. The bot signature record is the authoritative one once
it is in place; the pre-bot pull-request acknowledgment above is a
stopgap so contributions from this period are still on the record.

## What you are agreeing to (plain-English summary)

This is not the agreement — read the actual documents above for that —
but here is what they boil down to:

- You keep the copyright in your contribution.
- You grant the project maintainer a broad license to use, modify, and
  relicense your contribution under any terms, including AGPL-3.0,
  future open-source licenses, and proprietary or commercial licenses.
- You grant a matching patent license for any patents that would
  otherwise read on your contribution.
- You promise that what you are submitting is yours to submit and is
  not encumbered by conflicting claims, liens, or employer rights.
- Your contribution comes with no warranty.

## Source and legal review

The agreements in this directory are adapted from the
[Harmony Agreements](https://harmonyagreements.org/) Individual and
Corporate CLA templates, "any license" variant. They have been
customized for the py-mra project but have not yet been reviewed by an
attorney representing the maintainer. They are sufficient for
collecting signatures during the project's early phase, but should be
reviewed by counsel before any litigation or formal corporate
contribution.
